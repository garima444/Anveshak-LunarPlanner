Smart Landing Sites

AI-powered scoring ranks hundreds of candidate sites by safety, sunlight, slope, and mission objectives. Find the perfect spot!

Energy & Path Planning

A* pathfinder navigates real terrain. Bekker-Wong terramechanics models wheel sinkage. Solar recharge stops calculated automatically.

Select from 5 experiment modes — water ice, mineralogy, thermal gradients, geomorphology, space weathering. Real NASA data layers!

🌟 About the Project

Built for Lunar Explorers, by Future Engineers

Anveshak is an open-source lunar mission planning tool developed to make space exploration accessible for students, researchers, and space agencies. Named after the Sanskrit word for "explorer", it embodies India's spirit of discovery.

The tool integrates real NASA LOLA Digital Elevation Models, Bekker-Wong terramechanics, DBSCAN anomaly detection, and A* pathfinding into a single easy-to-use interface.
